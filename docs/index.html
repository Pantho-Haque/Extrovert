FAQ

Questions, answered.

Something else on your mind? Ask on GitHub; a person answers.

Ask a question
Is it really free?

Yes. Every feature, unlimited accounts, unlimited websites, any number of computers. Extrovert is open source under the MIT license, with no paid plan.

Does Extrovert change the sites or read my messages?

No. It shows each site exactly as it is and never changes or automates its pages. It only uses what any browser knows: page titles (for unread counts), the sites' own notifications, and which page you're on.

Why do I sign in with Google?

Each Google account becomes its own workspace, with its own logins. Google shares only your name, email and photo. Your Google apps (Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Meet, Chat) then open as that account.

Can I use two accounts of the same app?

Yes. Add WhatsApp twice, or three Gmails. They're grouped in the sidebar and tagged so you can tell them apart, and each keeps its own login.

Can I add apps that aren't in the list?

Any website. Paste its address; Extrovert keeps the site's own icon, or you can pick one from the built-in icon pack and give it any color.

Is "Sign in with your browser" safe?

It reads the logins of only the services you pick, from the browser and profile you pick, and only after macOS asks for your permission. Nothing is sent anywhere. If a copied login expires, Extrovert copies it again from the same browser; logging out of the service stops that. It works on macOS with Arc, Chrome, Brave, Edge, Vivaldi, Opera and Chromium.

Will I miss notifications?

Not if you keep Extrovert running: every service stays live in the background, with badges and native notifications. If a service signs you out, Extrovert tells you right away instead of quietly showing zero.

How much memory does it use?

Each service is a full web page, like a browser tab. Extrovert loads them one at a time, puts idle feeds to sleep when memory runs low, and lets you pause whole workspaces. The task manager shows what each service uses.

How do updates work without a server?

New versions are published on GitHub Releases. Extrovert checks there at startup and every 6 hours. On Windows and Linux it downloads the update and installs it when you restart; on macOS it gives you a one-click download of the new version. The check sends no personal data.

I found a bug. Where do I report it?

In the app: the ⌘ button in the sidebar → Report a problem. It fills in a GitHub issue for you; you need a free GitHub account to submit it. Or open one here.
