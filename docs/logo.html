Two takes on an extrovert.

Extrovert reaches out to every platform you use, the way an extrovert walks into a room and greets everyone. Hover a logo to see it say hi again; press Replay to watch it arrive.

A · The Hello Chosen

Arms thrown up, \o/, a hand out to each side: greeting everyone at once. Friendly, and simple enough for a 16-pixel menu bar.

Extrovert: all your communities

  • Motion: the head appears, the arms (or limbs) draw out, then each hand pops in, "hi… hi", followed by one wave (A) or jump (B). On hover the hands light up in WhatsApp green, LinkedIn blue, Instagram pink and Facebook blue, like messages arriving.
  • Menu bar: a one-color version with heavier strokes, so it stays clear at 18 px and macOS can tint it. The number next to it is your unread count.
  • Wordmark: lowercase "extrovert", next to the mark. Approachable, like the name.
  • Everywhere now: app icon, dock, Windows/Linux taskbar, menu-bar and tray icon, About panel, the logo inside the app, the website, favicon and README. All made from resources/icon.svg (pnpm icons).
